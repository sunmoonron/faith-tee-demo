Our story

It started at a skating rink.

Still Waters began with three shirts printed for friends at our Tuesday skate, and a question we kept hearing: where can I get one? Every design starts with a verse that steadied us, set in a hand we would actually wear. We keep the drops small, print to order, and give a tenth away.

Questions

Good to know

When do pre-orders close?

November 15 at midnight. We print once for the whole drop, so we cannot add orders after that. The next drop opens before Easter.

When will my order ship?

By December 1, with tracking by email. Canada takes 3 to 7 business days. US orders print in the US and usually arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

Do you ship to the US?

Yes, at the same flat rate, and with no customs charges, because US orders are printed and shipped inside the US.

What if the size is wrong?

Email us within 14 days and we send the right size. Return the first one unworn and we cover the label.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see or store your card number.

Which Bible translation do you use?

Short phrases and references, mostly in the wording of the King James Version, which is public domain. We keep quotes short on purpose.

Can my church or youth group order together?

Yes. Email hello@stillwatersapparel.ca with sizes and quantities for group pricing on 12 or more shirts, and we can set up a private order page.

Is tax included?

Prices are in Canadian dollars. HST is added at checkout for Canadian orders. US orders pay no Canadian tax.
